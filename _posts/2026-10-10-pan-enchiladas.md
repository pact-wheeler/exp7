## Ingredients

- 1-2 cans of chicken or shredded rotisserie chicken
- 1 normal can enchilada sauce
- 1 (16 oz or whatever) block cream cheese
- 1 can black beans (or kidney beans)
- 8ish flour tortillas
- shredded cheese

## Instructions

- mix everything besides the tortillas in a bowl that feels like right size in your heart
- lay down the tortillas in an aesthetically pleasing way, overlapping in the pan
- pour the filling in the center of your aesthetically pleasing tortillas leaving half of each tortilla uncovered, and half covered
- fold the uncovered half of each tortilla
- cook for 25 min at 400 degrees
- weigh down with a cookie sheet on top while cooking
